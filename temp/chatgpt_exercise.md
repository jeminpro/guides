Absolutely. At **40, 175 cm and 85 kg**, your BMI is about **27.8**, so getting to **77 kg** is a very reasonable first target. More importantly, I would make your goal **body recomposition** rather than simply "weight loss": lose ~8 kg of fat while building enough muscle and fitness that you look noticeably leaner and become stronger.

The NHS recommends at least **150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week plus strength work on at least 2 days**, and specifically notes that strength and aerobic work can be combined on the same day. ([nhs.uk][1])

## The approach I'd recommend for you

I **wouldn't** do a traditional bodybuilding-style "Monday chest / Tuesday legs / Wednesday cardio" split.

Because you're currently not very active and have several goals, I'd use:

**3 × full-body strength + 2 × cardio/running + daily walking + short mobility**

This gives you:

* 🔥 Fat loss
* 💪 Muscle and functional strength
* 🧗 Calisthenics ability
* 🏃 Better running endurance
* 🧘 Flexibility
* ❤️ Cardiovascular health
* 💤 Recovery days so you don't burn out

Your existing equipment is actually **very good** for this. You don't need a gym.

Start around:

# 🏋️ How hard should your sets be?

Don't take every set to absolute failure.

For most exercises:

### Finish with ~1–3 reps left in the tank.

For example, if you could theoretically do 13 push-ups but stop at 10, that's good.

As you become fitter:

**10 → 12 → 15 → harder variation**

rather than endlessly doing hundreds of repetitions.

The NHS recommends strength exercises around **8–12 repetitions**, generally at a level where another repetition would be difficult, and building gradually. ([nhs.uk][3])
