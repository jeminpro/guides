The daily 10-minute stretch

You don't need an hour of yoga. Nine stretches, 30–45 seconds each, done after training or in the evening — every day. Months of this quietly transforms your flexibility.

The routine — top to bottom

1 · Standing hamstring 30–45 s / leg

Heel on a low step, leg straight. Hinge forward from the hips with a flat back until the back of the thigh pulls.

2 · Hip flexor lunge 30–45 s / side

Half-kneel, back knee on the mat. Tuck the pelvis and shift forward until the front of the rear hip stretches. The desk-job antidote.

3 · Quad stretch 30–45 s / leg

Standing, pull one heel to your glutes, knees together, pelvis tucked. Hold the tower for balance.

4 · Calf stretch 30–45 s / leg

Hands on a wall, one leg back with the heel pressed down. Essential insurance for the running and skipping days.

5 · Figure-4 glute 30–45 s / side

On your back, ankle over the opposite knee, pull that thigh toward you until the outer hip releases.

6 · Chest doorway 30–45 s

Forearm on a door frame, elbow at shoulder height, step through gently. Undoes push-day and desk hunch.

7 · Lat stretch 30–45 s / side

Grab the tower upright, sit hips back and let the side of your back lengthen. Helps overhead pressing and pull-ups.

8 · Child's pose 45–60 s

Knees wide, hips to heels, arms long on the mat. Breathe slowly into the lower back.

9 · Thoracic rotation 8–10 / side

On all fours, hand behind head, rotate the elbow up toward the ceiling and follow it with your eyes. Frees the mid-back.

Rules of stretching

  • Mild tension, never pain — a 6/10 pull, not a grimace.
  • No bouncing. Ease in, breathe out, sink a little deeper.
  • Stretch warm muscles when you can — after workouts is prime time.
  • Consistency wins: 10 minutes daily beats 70 minutes on Sunday.

Where it fits in the week

  • Every day: this 10-minute routine, after training or before bed.
  • Thursday: extend to ~20 min of mat mobility/yoga after skipping HIIT.
  • Sunday: the full routine, unhurried, plus an easy walk — that's active recovery done.
Flexibility is a training effect, not a gift. Hold each stretch honestly, breathe, and expect visible change on a scale of months — exactly like strength. The squat, hinge and overhead positions in your workouts will all improve with it.
