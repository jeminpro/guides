Cardio, skipping & the running plan

Three cardio doses a week: an easy aerobic base on Tuesday, short hard skipping on Thursday, and one long endurance session on Saturday.

Tue — Zone-2 + core

25–35 min treadmill brisk walk or light jog at a pace where you could hold a conversation. This builds the engine that lets you run longer.

Core finisher: bicycle crunch 3 × 15 · lying leg raise 3 × 12 · plank 2 × 30 s → p.10

Thu — Skip-rope HIIT

30 s fast skipping / 30 s rest × 10–12 rounds. New to rope? Start at 20 s on / 40 s off and build.

Then straight into 20 min mobility/yoga on the mat → p.11. Skipping is high impact — soft knees, flat surface, supportive shoes.

Sat — Long endurance

Start at 30 min brisk walking + jogging; add 5 min every 1–2 weeks toward 60 min.

Incline walking mixed with jogging on the treadmill is easier on the knees than flat-out running and still burns plenty.

Run/walk progression — from intervals to continuous running

Don't try to run 30 minutes straight on day one. Repeat each stage until it feels comfortable before moving on — speed comes later, distance first.

Weeks 1–2
jog 1 min / walk 2 min × 8 (+5 min walk both ends)
Weeks 3–4
jog 2 min / walk 2 min × 7
Weeks 5–6
jog 3 min / walk 90 s × 7
Weeks 7–8
jog 5 min / walk 2 min × 5
Weeks 9–10
continuous jog 20–30 min
Beyond
30 → 40 min continuous · then a 5K · then further
jog — easy, conversational walk — brisk recovery

The 80/20 rule

Roughly 80% of your cardio should feel easy (you can talk), 20% hard (Thursday HIIT). Making every run a race is the classic way beginners burn out and stop improving. Your first objective is simply: run longer without getting exhausted.

Daily steps — get out of “sedentary”

Weeks 1–26,000 / day
Weeks 3–47,000 / day
Weeks 5+8,000–10,000 / day

Post-meal walks (p.14) do a lot of this for free. Missing 10k some days is fine — breaking up long sitting is the point.

Build gradually. You're coming from low activity — ramping running and skipping slowly is what keeps knees, calves and Achilles happy at 40. If a stage feels hard, repeat it for another week. There is no prize for finishing the ladder early.
