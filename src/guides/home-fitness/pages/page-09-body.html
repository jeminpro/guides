Leg exercises

The biggest muscles in your body — squats, hinges and single-leg work drive fat loss, real-world strength and injury-proof knees.

Goblet squat

quads · glutes · core one dumbbell
  1. Hold one dumbbell vertically against your chest, elbows pointing down.
  2. Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out. Sit down between your knees, chest proud, until thighs are near parallel.
  3. Drive through mid-foot and heels to stand tall, knees tracking over toes.
Heels glued down; elbows finish inside the knees at the bottom Depth first, weight second Knees caving inward, heels lifting, back rounding
Easier: bodyweight squat to a chair — stand up without hands Harder: heavier dumbbell · two dumbbells at shoulders · 2-s pause at the bottom

Dumbbell Romanian deadlift (RDL)

hamstrings · glutes · lower back · grip dumbbells
  1. Dumbbells resting on the front of your thighs, knees soft (not bent like a squat).
  2. Push your hips straight back and slide the weights down your legs with a flat back, until you feel a firm hamstring stretch (around mid-shin).
  3. Drive hips forward to stand, squeezing glutes at the top.
This is a hinge, not a squat — hips travel backwards Weights brush the legs the whole way Rounding the lower back or craning the neck up
Easier: lighter · stop at knee height while learning the hinge Harder: heavier · single-leg RDL (balance + hamstrings)

Reverse lunge

quads · glutes · balance bodyweight / dumbbells
  1. Stand tall. Step one leg back into a long stride.
  2. Lower the back knee toward the floor until both knees are near 90°.
  3. Push through the front heel to return to standing; alternate legs.
Torso tall, front knee stacked over the ankle Stepping back is kinder to knees than lunging forward Slamming the back knee down or taking tightrope-narrow steps
Easier: hold the tower for balance · shorter range Harder: dumbbells in hand · Bulgarian split squat (rear foot on a step)

Walking lunge

quads · glutes · core · balance dumbbells
  1. Dumbbells at your sides, take a long step forward.
  2. Lower until the back knee hovers just off the floor.
  3. Drive through the front heel and step the back leg through into the next stride.
Slight forward torso lean is fine — collapsing isn't Short choppy steps; letting the front knee dive past the toes
Easier: stationary or reverse lunges first Harder: heavier dumbbells · longer strides

Step-up

quads · glutes · balance sturdy step / stair
  1. Place your whole foot on a sturdy platform around knee height (bottom stair to start).
  2. Drive through that heel to stand tall on top — the top leg does all the work.
  3. Lower slowly, same leg controlling the descent. All reps one side, then swap.
Control the way down — that's where the strength is built Bouncing off the floor leg or letting the knee cave inward
Easier: lower step · fingertips on a rail Harder: dumbbells · higher step · 3-s lowers
