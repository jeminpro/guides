Bodyweight strength — the ladder

Calisthenics milestones from “not very active” to genuinely strong. Tick a level completely before chasing the next — that's real-world strength, not just bigger muscles.

Milestone levels

Level 1
  • 10 push-ups
  • 30-s dead hang
  • 30-s plank
  • 10 bodyweight squats
Level 2
  • 20 push-ups
  • 1 pull-up
  • 10 hanging knee raises
  • 15 lunges / leg
Level 3
  • 30 push-ups
  • 5 pull-ups
  • 15 hanging knee raises
  • 60-s plank
Level 4
  • 10 pull-ups
  • 30 push-ups fresh
  • 10 controlled hanging leg raises
  • Full tower dips
Advanced
  • Chin-ups & weighted pull-ups
  • Pistol squat progression
  • L-sit on the tower
  • Toes-to-bar

How to actually progress

  • Reps first, then harder variation: 10 → 12 → 15 clean reps, then upgrade the exercise instead of grinding to 50.
  • Grease the groove: for pull-ups, several easy submaximal sets spread through the day beat one exhausting session.
  • Test your level once a month, fresh, after a rest day — and log it (p.14).

Expectations at 40

  • Level 1 within weeks; Level 2 is a realistic 2–4 month target — the first pull-up is the big prize.
  • Losing weight makes every bodyweight move easier: each kilo lost is a free strength gain on the bar.
  • Tendons adapt slower than muscles. When something twinges, drop back a stage for a week — that's progress protection, not failure.
Why this matters: at ~77 kg with Level 3 skills you'll be able to lift, carry and move your own body with ease — the exact “strength + calisthenics” goal this plan is built around.
