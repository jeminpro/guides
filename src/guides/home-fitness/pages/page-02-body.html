How hard should sets feel?

Never to absolute failure. Stop each set with 1–3 reps left in the tank — if you could do 13 push-ups, stop at 10.

Then apply progressive overload — tiny weekly upgrades:

Week 1goblet squat 10 kg × 10
Week 210 kg × 12
Week 312 kg × 10
Week 412 kg × 12 → then up again

Once you own the top of a rep range, add weight or a harder variation — don't drift into endless high reps.

Training rules for consistency

  • Rest 60–90 seconds between sets so each set is high quality.
  • Strength and cardio sit on alternating days, giving muscles 48 hours to rebuild.
  • If you miss a workout, just pick up the next session — never cram two workouts into one day.
  • Log your weights and reps in a notebook or note app after each session.

Daily non-negotiables — every single day

Warm up 5 minEasy treadmill walk + joint circles before every session. Protects knees and lower back at 40.
