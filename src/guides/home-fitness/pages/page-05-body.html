Workout C — legs & core

Friday · 40–50 min · dumbbells, power tower, a sturdy step. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets.

Warm-up — 7 minutes

Treadmill easy walk · 3 min Hip circles × 10 each way Bodyweight squats × 15 Leg swings × 10 / leg Calf raises × 15 Easy skipping · 30 s

The session

1 Dumbbell squatGoblet or a dumbbell in each hand — the heaviest lift of the week lives here 3 × 12–15 → p.9
2 Walking lunge with dumbbellsLong controlled steps; torso tall — quads, glutes and balance together 3 × 10 / leg → p.9
3 Step-upSturdy platform or bottom stair — drive through the top-leg heel, don't push off the floor foot 3 × 10 / leg → p.9
4 Push-upSecond dose of the week — same standard as Monday, add reps when you can 3 × 10–12 → p.7
5 Dumbbell lateral raiseLight weight, strict form — shoulders that look and stay healthy 2 × 10–15 → p.7
6 Hanging leg raise progressionKnee raises until they're easy, then straighter legs — toes-to-bar is the long-term target 3 × 6–12 → p.10
7 Side plankHold both sides; add seconds each week 3 × 20–30 s / side → p.10

Cool-down — 5 min

Stretch hip flexors, quads and calves (p.11) — legs carry tomorrow's long endurance session, so give them a head start.

Sore legs are normal — sharp pain isn't

Expect dull muscle soreness for a day or two at first; it fades within a couple of weeks. Sharp joint pain is a stop signal — shorten the range, lighten the load, or swap the movement.
