Workout B — full body, pull focus

Wednesday · 40–50 min · power tower, dumbbells, mat. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets.

Warm-up — 7 minutes

Treadmill easy walk · 3 min Arm circles × 10 each way Dead hang · 2 × 10–15 s Bodyweight squats × 10 Hip hinges (no weight) × 10 Shoulder rotations × 10

The session

1 Pull-up progressionWherever you are on the ladder: dead hang → scapular pull → negatives → band-assisted → full reps. No reps yet? 3 × 20–30 s dead hangs count. 3 × max effort → p.8
2 One-arm dumbbell rowHand braced on the tower or a chair — pull elbow to hip, no twisting 3 × 10–12 / side → p.8
3 Dumbbell Romanian deadliftThe hip hinge — hamstrings, glutes and lower back. Your “lift heavy stuff safely” pattern. 3 × 10–12 → p.9
4 Reverse lungeStep back, not forward — easier on the knees while you build strength 3 × 10 / leg → p.9
5 Hanging knee raiseOn the tower — curl knees up without swinging; graduates to straight legs later 3 × 6–12 → p.10
6 Dumbbell bicep curlElbows pinned to ribs — no swinging the weight up 2 × 12 → p.8
7 Side plankBoth sides — obliques are half of a flat stomach 2 × 20–45 s / side → p.10

Finish — 10 min mobility

Run the daily stretch routine from p.11 straight after: hamstrings, hip flexors and lats will be warm and stretch far better now than cold.

Your power tower is the star

The pull-up bar is the single most valuable thing you own for the calisthenics goal. Touch it every session — even 3 dead hangs on a busy day keeps the grip and shoulders adapting.
