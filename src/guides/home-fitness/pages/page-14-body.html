Habits, tracking & tips

The small levers that decide whether the plan works — and the honest verdicts on the popular tricks.

Habit verdicts — what's worth your effort

DO IT

Walk 10–15 min after lunch and dinner. Helps blood sugar and digestion, adds ~2,000 easy steps, needs zero willpower once it's routine. The single best “trick” in this guide.

DO IT

NEAT — non-exercise movement. Stairs over lifts, stand during calls, park further away. Adds up to a meaningful weekly calorie burn without a single extra workout.

DO IT

Protect sleep: 7–9 h, consistent times. Short sleep raises hunger, saps training energy and stalls fat loss. Treat bedtime like a training session.

FINE

Coffee. Totally fine — great pre-workout, even. Just don't let it become coffee + sugar + cream + biscuits, because those calories add up silently.

SKIP

Apple cider vinegar in the morning. Evidence for fat loss is weak-to-mixed; at best a tiny appetite effect. It can irritate the stomach and erode tooth enamel. If you enjoy it: 1–2 tsp diluted in water, rinse after. Put the effort into walking, protein and sleep instead.

How to measure progress

  • Weight — daily, judged weekly: after the toilet, before breakfast, same clothing. Compare 7-day averages, never single days (e.g. 85.0, 84.8, 85.2, 84.7, 84.9, 84.6, 84.8 → avg ≈ 84.9).
  • Waist — once a week, same spot on the abdomen. Often moves when the scale sulks — it's your best belly-fat signal.
  • Photos — monthly, same pose, light and time of day.
  • Strength & running log: max push-ups, pull-up ladder rung, weights lifted, longest jog. These keep you motivated in flat scale-weeks.

Hydration & extras

  • 2–2.5 L water/day, more on heavy days. No detox drinks needed.
  • Use your resistance bands often — assisted pull-ups, rows, face pulls, and shoulder mobility.
  • Keep the skip rope and gripper visible — friction kills habits, visibility feeds them.

Safety at 40

  • Generally healthy with normal BP — still worth a GP check-up if it's been a while, before ramping the running.
  • Sharp pain = stop. Normal muscle burn and next-day soreness are fine; joint pain is information.
  • Build gradually — the NHS advice for returners is explicit: shorter bouts count, ramp week by week.
The mindset that makes it stick: you're not “exercising to lose 8 kg” — you're becoming a person who trains, walks after meals and sleeps properly. Weight loss is the side effect. Miss a day? Run the minimum viable day (p.2) and keep the streak alive.
