Push exercises

Chest, shoulders and triceps. Used in Workouts A and C. Master the “easier” version before chasing the “harder” one.

Push-up

chest · front shoulders · triceps · core bodyweight / mat
  1. Hands slightly wider than shoulders, directly under your chest. Body one straight line from ear to ankle.
  2. Lower your chest to just above the floor over ~2 seconds, elbows about 45° from your ribs.
  3. Press back up as one rigid plank — hips move with the shoulders, never lagging behind.
Squeeze glutes and brace abs before the first rep Full range — chest low, arms straight at the top Elbows flared to 90°, sagging hips, nodding head
Easier: hands on the power tower or a table (incline) — lower the height over weeks; or knee push-ups Harder: diamond hands · feet elevated (decline) · slow 3-s lowers

Dumbbell overhead press

shoulders · triceps · upper chest · core dumbbells
  1. Stand feet hip-width, a dumbbell at each shoulder, forearms vertical.
  2. Brace, then press straight up until arms are locked out and biceps are beside your ears.
  3. Lower under control back to the shoulders — that's one rep.
Ribs down, glutes tight — the torso stays a pillar Exhale on the way up Leaning back to grind out reps — that's your lower back begging you to go lighter
Easier: seated with back support · lighter dumbbells Harder: heavier · one arm at a time (core works overtime)

Dips — bench, then tower

lower chest · triceps · front shoulders bench / power tower
  1. Bench dips first: hands on a bench edge behind you, legs out front. Bend elbows to ~90°, press back up.
  2. On the tower: start with a straight-arm support hold, shoulders pressed down away from ears.
  3. Lower until upper arms are about parallel with a slight forward lean, then press back to lockout.
Shoulders down and back the whole way Add depth gradually over weeks Sinking neck into shoulders, or diving too deep too soon
Easier: bench dips with bent knees · band looped under knees on the tower Harder: full parallel-bar dips · slow negatives (only if your tower supports dips safely)

Dumbbell floor press

chest · triceps dumbbells + mat
  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, dumbbells pressed above your chest.
  2. Lower until your upper arms rest gently on the floor, elbows ~45° from ribs.
  3. Pause a beat, then press back up. No bench needed — the floor sets a safe depth.
Wrists stacked over elbows all the way Bouncing your arms off the floor between reps
Easier: lighter · one arm at a time Harder: heavier · 3-s lowers — your swap-in when push-ups need more load

Dumbbell lateral raise

side shoulders light dumbbells
  1. Stand tall, dumbbells at your sides, slight bend in the elbows.
  2. Lead with the elbows and raise out to the side, up to shoulder height — no higher.
  3. Lower slowly over 2 seconds; the way down is half the exercise.
Go lighter than feels impressive — strict beats heavy here Shrugging, swinging, or raising above shoulder height
Easier: seated · even lighter Harder: 1-s pause at the top · slow negatives
