Food — where the fat loss happens

Training builds the body; the calorie deficit reveals it. No crash diet, no banned foods — a moderate deficit you can hold for six months.

Calories — the starting numbers

Estimated maintenance~2,350–2,450 kcal/day
Your target1,900–2,100 kcal/day
Expected loss0.3–0.7 kg/week
Time to ~77 kg≈ 4–6 months

Treat these as estimates, not gospel. Watch your 7-day weight average (p.14) for 2–3 weeks: not falling → trim ~150 kcal; falling faster than 1 kg/week → add ~150 kcal. A modest deficit protects the muscle you're building.

Protein — the number-one lever

120–150 g per day, spread ~30–40 g per meal.

It keeps muscle while you lose fat and keeps you full on fewer calories.

Eggs Fish Chicken Lean meat Greek yoghurt Cottage cheese Dal & beans Paneer (measured) Tofu Whey (optional)

Plus ~30 g fibre/day from vegetables, fruit, legumes and whole grains.

The plate system — no weighing required

½ plate — vegetables/salad. Volume and fibre; eat these first. ¼ plate — protein. Fish, chicken, dal, paneer, eggs. ¼ plate — carbohydrate. Rice, chapati, oats, millets, potato — controlled, not eliminated.

Works for any cuisine: fish + rice + vegetables · chicken + chapati + salad · dal + rice + vegetables + yoghurt. Healthy fats (nuts, seeds, olive oil) in small portions round it out.

The 80/20 rule — including biryani day

  • 80% of food: protein, vegetables, fruit, whole grains, healthy fats. 20%: whatever you love.
  • Biryani day: have it — one plate not two, add raita and salad, skip the fried sides and dessert on the same day.
  • One dessert never ruined a plan; daily unnoticed calorie-dense extras do. Keep treats deliberate, not default.

Favour vs limit

Favour lean protein · vegetables · fruit · whole grains · nuts & seeds in moderation · water as the main drink

Limit sugary drinks · fried snacks · refined flour · alcohol · big meals right before bed · “coffee + sugar + biscuits” creep

Why not just diet harder? Crash deficits burn muscle and rebound. You'll look far better at 77 kg having strength-trained your way down than having starved your way there — same scale weight, completely different body.
