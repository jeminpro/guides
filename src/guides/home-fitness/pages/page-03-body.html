Workout A — full body, push focus

Monday · 40–50 min · dumbbells, power tower, mat. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets.

Warm-up — 7 minutes, never skip it

Treadmill easy walk · 3 min Arm circles × 10 each way Hip circles × 10 Bodyweight squats × 10 Walking lunges × 5 / side Shoulder rotations × 10 Easy jumping jacks · 20–30 s

The session

1 Goblet squatDumbbell hugged to chest — wakes up the whole lower body first 3 × 12–15 → p.9
2 Push-upCan't do 6 clean reps? Raise hands on the power tower or a table and lower the height over the weeks 3 × 8–12 → p.7
3 Dumbbell overhead pressStanding or seated — press to lockout without leaning back 3 × 10–12 → p.7
4 DipsBench dips first, band-assisted on the tower next, full dips later 3 × 6–10 → p.7
5 PlankStraight line ear-to-ankle; squeeze glutes; stop before the hips sag 3 × 30–45 s → p.10
6 Dead bugSlow and controlled — far more useful for a flat stomach than endless crunches 2 × 8–12 / side → p.10
7 Hand gripper finisherGrip strength pays off in pull-ups, rows and carrying heavy stuff 2 × 15–20 / hand → p.8

Cool-down — 5 min

Stretch what you just worked: chest doorway, shoulders, quads — 30–45 s each (how-tos on p.11). Finish with a 10-min easy walk if you have time.

Progression rule for this session

When you hit the top of a rep range on all sets with 1–3 reps still in the tank, upgrade next week: heavier dumbbell on squats and presses, lower hand-height or harder variation on push-ups and dips.

Form beats load. Every exercise above has a step-by-step card with visual illustrations in the exercise guide (pages 7–10) — read it before your first session.
