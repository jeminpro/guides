How to use this guide

  • Pages 2–6 tell you what to do each day. Stick them on the wall.
  • Every exercise has a → p.N reference to its how-to card in the exercise guide (pages 7–10).
  • Form first, weight second. Leave 1–3 reps in the tank on every set.
  • Coming from low activity? Build up gradually — week 1 should feel too easy. A quick GP check-up before ramping up running is sensible.
Consistency beats speed. Three average weeks in a row beat one perfect week followed by a burnout. Zero-activity days are the only real failure.
