Measures, time & SDT

Convert to matching units before calculating; speed = distance ÷ time.

Metric conversions

Length

10 mm = 1 cm
100 cm = 1 m
1000 m = 1 km

Mass

1000 mg = 1 g
1000 g = 1 kg
1000 kg = 1 tonne

Capacity

1000 ml = 1 litre
100 cl = 1 litre
1 ml = 1 cm³

Bigger → smaller: multiply. Smaller → bigger: divide. 3.2 km = 3200 m · 4500 g = 4.5 kg

Imperial & mixed units — exams usually print the conversion

Length

12 in = 1 ft · 3 ft = 1 yd
1 in ≈ 2.5 cm · 1 mile ≈ 1.6 km

Mass

16 oz = 1 lb · 14 lb = 1 stone
1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb · 1 lb ≈ 454 g

Capacity

8 pints = 1 gallon
1 pint ≈ 568 ml · 1 gallon ≈ 4.5 L

Time — clock facts

60 seconds = 1 minute · 60 minutes = 1 hour
24 hours = 1 day · 7 days = 1 week
365 days = 1 year (366 in a leap year)

Noon & midnight:

Noon = 12:00 midday = 12 pm
Midnight = 00:00 = 12 am
After noon, add 12 (1:30 pm = 13:30)

Elapsed time from 09:45 to 11:20: 09:45→10:00 = 15 min · 10:00→11:20 = 1 h 20 → total 1 h 35 min

Minutes → decimal hours

For speed questions, divide minutes by 60. Do not treat minutes as hundredths.

15m→0.25h30m→0.5h 45m→0.75h20m→0.33h

2 hours 30 minutes = 2.5 h (2 + 30÷60), never 2.3 h.

Money: £1 = 100p. Line up decimal points. £10 − £6.35 = £3.65 change

Speed, distance & time

Remember this

Speed = DistanceTime

120 km in 2.5 h → S = 1202.5 = 48 km/h

Keep units consistent. Convert minutes first.

Rearrange for D or T

Start from S = DT, then solve like algebra.

Distance: multiply both sides by T → D = S × T

48 km/h for 2.5 h → D = 48 × 2.5 = 120 km

Time: divide both sides by S (or flip) → T = DS

120 km at 48 km/h → T = 12048 = 2.5 h
