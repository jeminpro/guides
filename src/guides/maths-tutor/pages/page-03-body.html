+ works both ways

Swap the numbers round — same answer! So you only learn half the square.

3 + 8 = 11 8 + 3 = 11

Take away undoes add

They are opposites — one fact gives you four.

7 + 8 = 15 8 + 7 = 15 15 − 8 = 7 15 − 7 = 8

Bonds to 10 and 100

Learn the pairs that make 10 — the same pairs make 100 (tens to 90, ones to 10).

1+9 2+8 3+7 4+6 5+5 37 + 63 = 100

Adding tricks

Bigger first Start from the larger number3 + 9 → 9 + 3 = 12
Make 10 first Split to fill up to 108 + 5 → 8 + 2 + 3 = 13
Doubles Learn all 9, they unlock the rest7 + 7 = 14
Near doubles Double, then fix it by 16 + 7 → 12 + 1 = 13
+9 / +8 Add 10, then take 1 or 2 off6 + 9 → 16 − 1 = 15
Spot a 10 Three numbers? Pair to 107 + 4 + 3 → 10 + 4 = 14

Take-away tricks

Add it back Make it a missing-number sum15 − 8 → 8 + ? = 15
Count up Numbers close? Hop up instead15 − 13 → +2 = 2
Back through 10 Drop to 10, then carry on15 − 7 → 15 − 5 − 2 = 8
−9 / −8 Take 10, then give 1 or 2 back16 − 9 → 6 + 1 = 7
Halves Doubles backwards14 − 7 = 7
Same difference Shift both to a tidy number82 − 29 → 83 − 30 = 53
