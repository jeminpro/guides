Multiplication (×)

  • Ignore the decimal for now: work with 258 × 75.
  • Multiply by the ones digit (5) → get 1290.
  • Multiply by the tens digit (7), put a 0 on the end first → get 18060.
  • Add those two answers together.
  • Count decimal places in the starting numbers (here: 1) and put the point that many digits from the right.

Division (÷)

117 ÷ 5 = 1175
  • How many times does 5 fit into the first digits? (11 ÷ 5 = 2)
  • Write that on top. Multiply (2 × 5 = 10), then subtract.
  • Bring down the next digit and do the same again.
  • Still some left over? Add a decimal point and a 0, then keep going.
  • When nothing is left over, you’re done - line the decimal points up!

Multiplying & dividing in your head

1 · Doubling & halving
Double it, then double again

×4 = double twice · ×8 = double 3 times · ÷4 = halve twice.

35 × 4 → 70 → 140  ·  96 ÷ 4 → 48 → 24
2 · Partitioning
Split it up, then add

Split into tens and ones, times each part, then add them.

23 × 6 → (20×6) + (3×6) → 120 + 18 = 138
3 · × and ÷ by 10, 100, 1000
Move the point, or cancel zeros

×10 / ×100 / ×1000: digits move 1 / 2 / 3 places left. ÷10 / ÷100 / ÷1000: digits move 1 / 2 / 3 places right (the decimal point shifts).

4710 = 4.7 47100 = 0.47 471000 = 0.047
4.7 × 100 = 470  ·  30 × 40 = 1200

Zeros: write it as a fraction and cancel matching zeros on top and bottom.

35010 = 35 4800100 = 48 60001000 = 6
4 · Related facts
Use a fact you already know

×5 = ×10 then halve · ×9 = ×10 then take one lot off · ×11 = ×10 then add one lot · ÷5 = ÷10 then double.

28 × 5 → 280 ÷ 2 = 140 7 × 9 → 70 − 7 = 63 14 × 11 → 140 + 14 = 154 45 ÷ 5 → 4.5 × 2 = 9
