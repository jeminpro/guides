Addition & Subtraction

Addition (+)

  • Line ones under ones. Start on the right.
  • Ones: 7 + 6 = 13 → write 3, carry 1.
  • Tens: 1 + 8 + 5 = 14 → write 4, carry 1.
  • Hundreds: 1 + 4 + 3 = 8.

Subtraction (−)

  • Line ones under ones. Start on the right.
  • Ones: borrow 1 ten from the 4. The 4 becomes 3, and the 3 becomes 13. Then 13 − 6 = 7.
  • Tens: 3 is too small, so borrow 1 hundred from the 8. The 8 becomes 7, and the 3 becomes 13. Then 13 − 5 = 8.
  • Hundreds: 7 − 3 = 4.

Learn these by heart

Learn quick addition and subtraction of single digits (0 to 9).

6 + 7 = 13 9 − 4 = 5

Also learn quick take-aways from (10 to 19), taking away a single digit (0 to 9).

15 − 8 = 7 13 − 6 = 7

Adding & subtracting in your head

1 · Partitioning
Split into tens and ones

Add the tens, add the ones, then join them.

47 + 36 → (40+30) + (7+6) → 70 + 13 = 83

When: any 2-digit add where neither number is near a 10.

53 + 28 = 81 64 + 25 = 89 35 + 47 = 82
2 · Compensation
Use a tidy number, then give the change back

Round to the nearest 10 to make it easy, then undo exactly what you changed.

198 + 47 → 200+47 = 247 → −2 = 245 82 − 29 → 82−30 = 52 → +1 = 53

When: one number is just below a tidy 10 or 100.

299 + 46 = 345 97 + 38 = 135 81 − 39 = 42
3 · Bridging
Hop to the next 10 first

Jump up to the next 10, then add what is left.

38 + 7 → 38+2 = 40 → 40+5 = 45

When: the ones take you past a 10.

47 + 8 = 55 26 + 7 = 33 58 + 6 = 64
4 · Difference method
Count up instead of taking away

Start at the smaller number and count up to the bigger one. Add the hops.

63 − 58 58 →+2 60 →+3 63 2 + 3 = 5

When: the two numbers are only a little apart.

72 − 67 = 5 51 − 48 = 3 84 − 79 = 5
