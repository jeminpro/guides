Odd, even & clever tricks

Even numbers end in 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8; odd numbers end in 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9.

Odd and even numbers

Odd

Share them into 2 equal groups and there is always 1 left over (not divisible by 2).

●●●●○

5 → 2 pairs and 1 with no partner

1   3   5   7   9

Just check the last digit — odd numbers end in these

Even

Share them into 2 equal groups and there is nothing left over (divisible by 2).

●●●●●●

6 → 3 pairs, everyone has a partner

0   2   4   6   8

Ends in these — and 0 counts as even too

Adding & taking away

RuleExample
Odd + Odd = Even3 + 5 = 8
Even + Even = Even2 + 4 = 6
Odd + Even = Odd5 + 2 = 7

Two spare ones pair up — that is why odd + odd is even.

Multiplying

RuleExample
Odd × Odd = Odd5 × 7 = 35
Odd × Even = Even5 × 8 = 40
Even × Even = Even4 × 8 = 32

If either number is even, the answer is even.

Adding & subtracting in your head

1 · Partitioning
Split into tens and ones

Add the tens, add the ones, then join them.

47 + 36 → (40+30) + (7+6) → 70 + 13 = 83
2 · Compensation
Use a tidy number, then give the change back

Round to the nearest 10 to make it easy, then undo exactly what you changed.

198 + 47 → 200+47 = 247 → −2 = 245 82 − 29 → 82−30 = 52 → +1 = 53
3 · Bridging
Hop to the next 10 first

Jump up to the next 10, then add what is left.

38 + 7 → 38+2 = 40 → 40+5 = 45
4 · Difference method
Count up instead of taking away

Start at the smaller number and count up to the bigger one. Add the hops.

63 − 58 58 →+2 60 →+3 63 2 + 3 = 5

Which trick should I use?

Any 2-digit add Partition. Neither number is near a 10, so split both into tens and ones, add each part, then join. 53 + 28 = 81 64 + 25 = 89 35 + 47 = 82
Near a 10 Compensate. One number is just below a tidy 10 or 100, so round it up, add, then take the extra back off. 299 + 46 = 345 97 + 38 = 135 81 − 39 = 42
Crossing 10 Bridge. The ones take you past a 10, so hop up to that 10 first, then add whatever is left. 47 + 8 = 55 26 + 7 = 33 58 + 6 = 64
Numbers close Count up. The two numbers are only a little apart, so count on from the smaller one instead of taking away. 72 − 67 = 5 51 − 48 = 3 84 − 79 = 5
