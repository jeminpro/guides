4 · The three percentage questions

One formula for all three: %100 = partwhole. Write in the two numbers you know, cross-multiply, then divide to find the letter.

Find the percentage

5 is what % of 20?
p100 = 520
20 × p = 5 × 100 = 500
p = 500 ÷ 20 = 25%

Find the part

What is 25% of 64?
25100 = n64
100 × n = 64 × 25 = 1600
n = 1600 ÷ 100 = 16

Find the whole

15 is 20% of what?
20100 = 15w
20 × w = 15 × 100 = 1500
w = 1500 ÷ 20 = 75

5 · Increase, decrease & percentage change

Increase by X% → final is (100 + X)%. Decrease by X% → final is (100 − X)%.

+10% → ×1.10+25% → ×1.25−15% → ×0.85

Measure percentage change from the ORIGINAL: change ÷ original × 100.

£40 → £50: change £10 · original £40 → 10 ÷ 40 = 25% increase (not ÷ 50)

Changes do not simply add: −10% twice gives 0.9 × 0.9 = 0.81, so 19% off.
