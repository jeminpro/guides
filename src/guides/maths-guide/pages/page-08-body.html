Factors, multiples & primes

Factors divide exactly; multiples belong to a times table; a prime has exactly two factors.

Factors

Numbers that divide exactly into another.

Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

Multiples

The times-table answers of a number.

Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16…

Primes

Exactly two factors: 1 and itself.

2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17…

1 is not prime. 2 is the only even prime.

Composite number The opposite of a prime: more than two factors. 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12… 1 is neither prime nor composite.
Index notation The small raised number counts how many of that factor you multiply. 2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 Read 2³ as “2 to the power 3”. 3² is “3 squared”.
Factors of a square Square numbers always have an odd number of factors. Non-square numbers always have an even number of factors. 36 has 9 factors (odd) 12 has 6 factors (even)

Prime factors

To find the prime factors, draw a factor tree (circle primes, keep splitting composites), or keep dividing by 2, 3, 5, 7… until you reach 1.

30 = 2 × 3 × 5 84 → 2×42 → 2×2×21 → 2² × 3 × 7

HCF — Highest Common Factor

Biggest number that divides into both. Two ways — same answer.

Way 1 · list the factors, ring the biggest shared one

18 → 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
24 → 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24
Shared: 1, 2, 3, 6 → HCF = 6

Way 2 · prime factors

18 = 2 × 3²  ·  24 = 2³ × 3
Shared primes, lowest power → 2 × 3 = 6

LCM — Lowest Common Multiple

Smallest number that is a multiple of both. Two ways — same answer.

Way 1 · list the multiples, stop at the first match

18 → 18, 36, 54, 72
24 → 24, 48, 72
First one in both lists → LCM = 72

Way 2 · prime factors

18 = 2 × 3²  ·  24 = 2³ × 3
Every prime, highest power → 2³ × 3² = 72

Exam tip: Listing is quickest for small numbers; prime factors win for big ones. Check your pair with HCF × LCM = a × b (6 × 72 = 432 = 18 × 24).
