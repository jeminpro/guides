Averages & charts

Three averages and the range

Mean
Add all values, then divide by how many there are

The mean gives the equal-share value.

2, 5, 5, 8 → 2+5+5+84 = 5
Median
Put in order, then find the middle

Odd number of values: use the one middle value. Even number: add the two middle values and divide by 2.

1, 3, 5 → one middle → median = 3 1, 3, 5, 9 → (3+5)÷2 → median = 4
Mode
The value or values that appear most

If values tie for the highest frequency, they are all modes. If every value appears equally often, there is no mode.

2, 2, 4, 4, 7 → modes = 2 and 4 1, 2, 3, 4 → no mode
Range
Biggest − smallest

This shows the spread — it is not an average.

2, 5, 5, 8 → 8 − 2 = 6
Three averages, one spread: mean, median and mode are the averages — range only measures spread. Mean is mean — it makes you do the adding and dividing · Median is the middle · Mode is the most often.

Reading charts

Bar chart

Height of each bar = frequency / value. Check the scale and whether bars start at zero.

Line graph

Best for change over time. Read both axes carefully — join the plotted points.

Pie chart

Full circle = 360° = whole set. Angle = parttotal × 360°.

12/36 × 360° = 120°

Pictogram

Always check the key. Half a symbol = half the key value.
