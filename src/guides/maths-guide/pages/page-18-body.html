Coordinates & transformations

Coordinates are written (x, y): move across the x-axis first, then up or down the y-axis.

Coordinates (x, y)

Along the corridor (x), then up/down the stairs (y).

(3, −2) → right 3, down 2

Origin = (0, 0). Count from O along x, then parallel to y.

Line symmetry

A line of symmetry is a mirror line: if you fold along it, both halves match exactly.

A circle has infinitely many lines of symmetry.

Transformations

Translation

Slide — no turn, no flip. Describe it with a column vector: top = across, bottom = up/down.

3−2 means (x, y) → (x + 3, y − 2)

Reflection

Flip — same distance the other side of the mirror. Always name the mirror line.

In the y-axis: (a, b) → (−a, b)
