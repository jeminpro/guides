Shape cheats

Perimeter is the distance around a shape; area is the space inside it, measured in square units.

Square

Area = s²Perimeter = 4s
  • All sides equal
  • Opposite sides parallel
  • All interior angles 90°
  • Lines of symmetry 4 · rotational order 4

Rectangle

Area = a × bPerimeter = 2a + 2b
  • Opposite sides equal & parallel
  • Adjacent interior angles 90°
  • Lines of symmetry 2 · rotational order 2

Rhombus

Area = b × hPerimeter = 4b
  • All sides equal; opposite sides parallel
  • Opposite angles equal; diagonals cross at 90°
  • Lines of symmetry 2 · rotational order 2

Parallelogram

Area = b × hPerimeter = 2a + 2b
  • Opposite sides equal & parallel (a = slant side)
  • Opposite angles equal; h ⟂ b, not the slant
  • Lines of symmetry 0 · rotational order 2

Kite

Area = d₁ × d₂2Perimeter = 2a + 2b
  • Two pairs of adjacent sides equal; only one pair of opposite angles equal
  • Diagonals cross at 90°; the long one is the mirror line
  • Lines of symmetry 1 · rotational order 1

Trapezium

Area = a + b2 × hPerimeter = sum of sides
  • One pair of opposite sides parallel (a and b)
  • Isosceles (shown) — slant sides equal, base angles equal, 1 line of symmetry
  • Plain trapezium: symmetry 0 · rotational order 1

Triangle

Area = b × h2Perimeter = sum of sides
  • Equilateral — all sides equal
  • Isosceles — 2 sides equal
  • Scalene — no sides equal
  • Acute — all angles less than 90°
  • Obtuse — one angle greater than 90°

Circle

Area = πr²Circumference = 2πr = πd
  • Diameter = 2 × radius (2r)
  • π ≈ 227 ≈ 3.14

Right-angled triangle

Pythagoras: a² + b² = c² Area = a × b2
  • One angle is exactly 90° (square corner)
  • Hypotenuse c is longest — opposite the right angle
  • Other two angles add to 90°

Semicircle

Area = πr²2 Perimeter = πr + d
  • Half a circle (diameter is the straight edge)
  • Perimeter = curved part + diameter
  • Diameter = 2r · π ≈ 3.14
