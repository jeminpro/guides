Volume formulas

Cuboid: V = length × width × height

5 × 3 × 2 = 30 cm³ · Cube: V = s³

Prism: V = area of cross-section × length

Triangle end 12 cm² × 10 cm = 120 cm³

Cylinder: V = πr²h  (or area of circle × height)

π × 3² × 10 ≈ 282.7 cm³
Surface area (cuboid): Add the areas of all faces: 2(lw + lh + wh). Don’t mix area (cm²) with volume (cm³).
