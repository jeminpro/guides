How questions use squares & roots

Area of a square → side
Side = √area

A square rug covers 49 m². How long is one side?

Side = √49 = 7 m · perimeter = 4 × 7 = 28 m
Volume of a cube → edge
Edge = ∛volume

A cube-shaped box holds 125 cm³. How long is one edge?

Edge = ∛125 = 5 cm because 5 × 5 × 5 = 125
A root written in words
“What is the square root of 144?”

Turn it into a times question: which number times itself makes 144?

12 × 12 = 144 → √144 = 12
Estimate a root
Trap it between two squares you know

√50 is not a whole number, so find the squares either side of 50.

7² = 49 √50 ≈ 7.1 8² = 64
50 is just past 49, so √50 is between 7 and 8, much closer to 7
