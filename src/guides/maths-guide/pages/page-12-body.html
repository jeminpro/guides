Fraction skills

Improper fraction Top bigger than (or equal to) the bottom, so it is worth 1 or more. 114, 77
Mixed number A whole number written next to a proper fraction. 234 means 2 + 34
Reciprocal Flip a fraction upside down — that is simply swapping the top number and the bottom number. 45 → 54, 3 → 13

Simplify

Divide top & bottom by the same number (HCF).

12 ÷ 618 ÷ 6 → 23

Equivalent fractions

Multiply (or divide) top & bottom by the same number.

23 = 46 = 69

Mixed → improper

Whole × denominator + numerator, keep denominator.

234 → (2×4)+34 = 114

Improper → mixed

Divide top by bottom: the whole number goes in front, the remainder becomes the new top.

114 → 11 ÷ 4 = 2 r 3 → 234

Fraction of an amount

“of” means multiply.

Write it as one fraction, then divide before you multiply.

34 of 48 → 3 × 484 → 3 × 12 → 36

Order & compare

Same bottom → larger top wins.
Same top → smaller bottom wins.
Otherwise, make the bottoms match.

27 < 57 25 > 27 23 vs 35 → 1015 vs 915 → 23 larger

Add & subtract

Same denominator
Add/subtract tops only
27 + 37 = 57
Different denominators
Make bottoms match (LCM)
13 + 16 = 26 + 16 = 36 = 12
Mixed numbers
Make improper first, then match bottoms
234 + 112 = 114 + 64 = 174 = 414 314 − 134 = 134 − 74 = 112

Multiply & divide

Multiply
Tops × tops, bottoms × bottoms
23 × 45 = 815
Cancel before you multiply
Use smaller numbers
34 × 89 4 & 8 ÷ 4 → 31 × 29 3 & 9 ÷ 3 → 11 × 23 = 23
Divide
Flip the second fraction, then multiply
23 ÷ 45 = 23 × 54 = 56
