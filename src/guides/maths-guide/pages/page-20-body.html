Useful facts

1 is neither prime nor composite

A prime has exactly two factors and is greater than 1. The number 1 has only one factor — itself — so it is neither. Negatives, fractions and decimals are never prime.

0 is neither positive nor negative

A positive number is greater than 0. A negative number is less than 0. Zero sits between them, so it is neither.

2 is the only even prime

2 is prime. Every other even number divides by 2, so it has more than two factors and is composite.

0 is even — never divide by 0

0 ends in 0, so it is even. 0 × 7 = 0, so it is a multiple of every whole number, and 0 ÷ 7 = 0. Dividing by 0 has no answer.

1 divides every whole number

1 is a factor of every whole number, and every whole number is a factor of itself. Both 1 and 0 are squares and cubes.

Pictures are not to scale

Do not judge a length or an angle by how big it looks. Read the labels and use the rules.

Match the form they ask for

If it says centimetres, m², or 24-hour time, convert first. The right number in the wrong form does not score.

¾ = 0.75 = 75%

Turn a fraction, a decimal and a percentage into the same type before you compare them. ¾ is greater than 0.6.

An hour has 60 minutes

1.5 hours = 1 hour 30 minutes, not 1 hour 50 minutes. 2 hours 15 minutes = 135 minutes.

Further below zero is smaller

−7 is less than −2. The gap from −7°C to 9°C is 16 degrees, because the jump passes through 0.
