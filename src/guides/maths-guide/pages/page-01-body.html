Compare numbers

1

Count the digits. For whole numbers, more digits means bigger. 5,847 vs 962 → 5,847 is greater

2

Same length? Compare left to right - start with the leftmost digit. The first place they differ decides which is bigger.

3

Decimals? If the whole parts match, compare after the point left to right - tenths, hundredths… The first place they differ decides which is greater. 0.58 vs 0.6 → 0.6 is greater

Rounding

1
Find the place

Spot the place value you’re rounding to - ones, tens, hundreds… Circle that digit.

2
Look next digit

Check the digit immediately to its right - it decides everything.

3
Decide

0–4 keep the circled digit · 5–9 add 1 to it.

4
Change everything after

Whole numbers: turn digits after into 0
Decimals: remove those digits

Odd and even numbers

Even numbers end in 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8 (0 counts).
Odd numbers end in 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9.
Check the last digit only.

Adding & taking away

Same type (odd,odd or even,even) → even.
Mixed (odd,even or even,odd) → odd.

3 + 5 = 8 2 + 4 = 6 5 + 2 = 7

Multiplying

If either number is even, the answer is even.
Odd × odd stays odd.

5 × 8 = 40 4 × 8 = 32 5 × 7 = 35
