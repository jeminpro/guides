Further right always wins
Further right = the bigger number, even for negatives. −7 < −3  ·  −1 < 2
Careful with negatives
Bigger digit, but further left — so −7 is smaller. order: −7, −3, −1, 0, 2
+ goes right, − goes left
Adding hops right, taking away hops left. −2 + 5 = 3  ·  3 − 5 = −2

Adding positive & negative integers

Signs Operation Answer sign Example
+  + Add Positive 2 + 5 = 7
−  − Add Negative −2 + (−5) = −7
+  − Subtract Sign of the bigger number 2 − 5 = −3  ;  2 − 1 = 1
−  + Subtract Sign of the bigger number −2 + 5 = 3  ;  −2 + 1 = −1

Two signs next to each other

Adding a negative → go left
5 + (−3) = 5 − 3 = 2 −4 + (−3) = −4 − 3 = −7
Taking away a negative → go right
5 − (−3) = 5 + 3 = 8 −4 − (−3) = −4 + 3 = −1

Why is 5 − (−3) = 8 and not 2? Two minuses side by side always turn into a plus, so start at 5 and hop 3 to the right.

Multiplying & dividing — one rule for both

Same signs → Positive
+ × + = +    2 × 5 = 10 − × − = +    (−2) × (−5) = 10 + ÷ + = +    10 ÷ 2 = 5 − ÷ − = +    (−10) ÷ (−2) = 5
Different signs → Negative
+ × − = −    2 × (−5) = −10 − × + = −    (−2) × 5 = −10 + ÷ − = −    10 ÷ (−2) = −5 − ÷ + = −    (−10) ÷ 2 = −5
