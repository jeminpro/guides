Divisibility rules

Check the last digit for 2, 5 or 10; the digit sum for 3 or 9; the last two digits for 4.

Find every factor — stop when the pair meets

Test 1, 2, 3, 4… in order. Each “yes” gives a factor pair — a smaller number × a bigger number.

As you count up, the first number grows and the second shrinks. Stop when they meet (both equal, e.g. 6 × 6 for 36) or cross over (the first becomes bigger than the second). After that, pairs just repeat backwards — 12 × 7 is the same as 7 × 12, so you find no new factors.
