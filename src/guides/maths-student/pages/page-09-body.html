Coordinates (x, y)

Along the corridor (x), then up or down the stairs (y).

(3, −2) → right 3, down 2

Origin = (0, 0). Translation: slide. Reflection: flip. Rotation: turn.

Remember: Look for parallel arrows before using F, Z or C. Volume is cm³ — don’t mix it with area (cm²).
