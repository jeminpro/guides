Negatives & BIDMAS

Numbers get bigger to the right. For mixed calculations, use BIDMAS.

The number line

← smaller bigger →
−5−4−3−2−1012345

Further right always wins — even for negatives. −7 < −3 · Add hops right, take-away hops left.

Two signs next to each other

+ (−) → go left
5 + (−3) = 5 − 3 = 2
− (−) → go right
5 − (−3) = 5 + 3 = 8

Multiplying & dividing

Same signs → positive
(−2) × (−5) = 10
Different signs → negative
2 × (−5) = −10
