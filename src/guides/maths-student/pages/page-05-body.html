Factors, primes & squares

Factors divide exactly. Multiples sit in a times table. A prime has exactly two factors.

Factors

Numbers that divide exactly into another.

Multiples

The times-table answers of a number.

Primes

Exactly two factors: 1 and itself.

2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13…

1 is not prime. 2 is the only even prime.

HCF — Highest Common Factor

Biggest number that divides into both.

18 → 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
24 → 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24
Shared: 1, 2, 3, 6 → HCF = 6

LCM — Lowest Common Multiple

Smallest number in both times tables.

18 → 18, 36, 54, 72
24 → 24, 48, 72
First match → LCM = 72
