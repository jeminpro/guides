2D shapes & area

Perimeter is the distance around. Area is the space inside, in square units.

Square

Area = s²Perimeter = 4s
  • All sides equal · all angles 90°

Rectangle

Area = a × bPerimeter = 2a + 2b
  • Opposite sides equal · all angles 90°

Triangle

Area = b × h2Perimeter = sum of sides
  • Use perpendicular height, not a slant side

Parallelogram

Area = b × hPerimeter = 2a + 2b
  • Height is perpendicular to the base

Circle

Area = πr²Circumference = πd = 2πr
  • Diameter d = 2r · π ≈ 3.14
Remember: Area uses the perpendicular height. Units: perimeter in cm, area in cm².
