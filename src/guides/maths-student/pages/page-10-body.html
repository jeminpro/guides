Averages & word problems

Mean, median and mode are averages. Range is the spread — not an average.

Four words to know

Mean
Add up, then divide by how many
2, 5, 5, 8 → 20 ÷ 4 = 5
Median
Put in order, then pick the middle
1, 3, 5 → 3 · even count: average the two middles
Mode
The value that appears most
2, 2, 4, 7 → mode = 2
Range
Biggest − smallest
2, 5, 5, 8 → 8 − 2 = 6

Reading charts

Bar chart

Height of each bar is the value. Check the scale.

Pie chart

Whole circle = 360°. Bigger slice = bigger share.

Pictogram

Always read the key. Half a symbol = half the key.
