Ratio, measures & time

Simplify a ratio by dividing every part equally. Convert units before you calculate.

What is a ratio?

Compares two or more amounts. Order matters.

Boys : girls = 3 : 2 → for every 3 boys there are 2 girls

Simplify

Divide every part by the HCF.

12 : 18 → 2 : 3

Sharing in a ratio

Count the parts first
Total parts → one part → each share

Share £56 in the ratio 3 : 4

Parts = 7 · one part = 56 ÷ 7 = 8 · shares = £24 : £32

Metric conversions

Length

10 mm = 1 cm
100 cm = 1 m
1000 m = 1 km

Mass

1000 mg = 1 g
1000 g = 1 kg
1000 kg = 1 tonne

Capacity

1000 ml = 1 litre
100 cl = 1 litre
1 ml = 1 cm³

Bigger → smaller: multiply. Smaller → bigger: divide. 3.2 km = 3200 m

Clock facts

60 seconds = 1 minute · 60 minutes = 1 hour
24 hours = 1 day · 7 days = 1 week

After noon, add 12. 1:30 pm = 13:30

Speed, distance, time

Speed = DistanceTime

Distance = S × T · Time = D ÷ S

120 km in 2.5 h → 48 km/h
Remember: Units must match before you add, share, or use SDT. 2 hours 30 minutes = 2.5 h, never 2.3 h.
